Single-Aisle Aircraft

Airbus AirbusA220-300 & Boeing 737 Max-7

The smallest single-aisle aircraft are designed for short-to-medium routes with lower passenger demand, offering airlines flexibility on thinner routes. Airbus's entry in this category is the A220, originally developed by Bombardier as the C Series before Airbus acquired majority ownership in 2018. The A220 is known for its wide cabin, large windows, and fuel efficiency, making it popular for regional and domestic routes. Boeing's counterpart is the 737 MAX 7, the smallest variant of the MAX family, built to compete directly with the A220 while sharing commonality with the rest of the 737 fleet.
